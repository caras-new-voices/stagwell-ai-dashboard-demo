## 1.2 CMO red flags (from the data)
1. **Organic keyword base shrinking:** -5.6% WW, -5.04% US this month; likes on IG recovering (+22.21%) but IG follower count slightly negative.
2. **Backlink toxicity HIGH:** 19.3% of analyzed backlinks toxic (3.6K links from spam blogs targeting Jordan/Nike terms) — brand-safety and SEO risk.
3. **Worst-in-class social audience quality:** IMAI Audience Quality Score 14 (Poor) vs Adidas/NB 43, Lululemon 40; 21.65% fake/bot followers — highest in the competitive set.
4. **Engagement rate laggard:** 0.06% IG ER vs Adidas 0.28%; Nike "hides likes" flag.
5. **Missing brand-term coverage:** Not ranking for high-volume rival brand terms (new balance 1.83M vol, under armour 1.22M, uggs, birkenstock) that retail partners capture.
6. **AI-search dependence on 3rd parties:** reddit.com (83.1K) and youtube.com (75.5K) are cited nearly as often as nike.com (83.5K) in AI answers.
